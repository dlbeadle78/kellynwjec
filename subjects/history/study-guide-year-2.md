# WJEC A Level History - Year 2 Study Guide

Kellyn's confirmed Year 2 route:

- Unit 3 Option 8 - The American Century c.1890-1990
- Unit 4 Option 3 - Reform and Protest in Wales and England, Part 2: Protest and Campaigns for Social Reform c.1832-1848
- Unit 5 - NEA / historical enquiry

The guide covers Civil Rights, the making of a superpower, Chartism, Welsh protest including Newport and Rebecca, the Anti-Corn Law League, Peel and Conservative transformation, Whig reform, factory/mines/public-health reform, primary-source evaluation, chronology, retrieval and revision routines.
