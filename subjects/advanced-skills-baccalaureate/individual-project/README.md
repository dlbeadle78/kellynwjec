# Individual Project

## What this is

The main current priority. It is an independent research project and is worth 50% of the qualification.

Kellyn must complete and evidence her own assessed work.

## Simple route through the project

1. Read the task and school instructions.
2. Confirm the approved topic or question with the supervisor.
3. Plan the research and deadlines.
4. Find, record and evaluate information.
5. Produce the written project or artefact required by the approved proposal.
6. Review the work against the task and mark scheme.
7. Complete the required records and authentication.

- Follow the school's rules on referencing, assistance and authentication.
