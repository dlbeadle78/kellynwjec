# WJEC Level 3 Advanced Skills Baccalaureate Wales - Study Guide

Kellyn's study guide covers the full qualification with particular focus on the Individual Project.

## Main areas

- Global Community Project - 25%
- Future Destinations Project - 25%
- Individual Project - 50%
- Planning and Organisation
- Critical Thinking and Problem Solving
- Creativity and Innovation
- Personal Effectiveness
- Literacy, Numeracy and Digital Competence
- research planning, source evaluation, ethics and referencing
- supervisor meetings, evidence trail and self-evaluation
