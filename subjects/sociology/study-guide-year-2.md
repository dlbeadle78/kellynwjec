# WJEC A Level Sociology - Year 2 Study Guide

Kellyn's Year 2 study guide covers:

- Unit 3 - Power and Control: Crime and Deviance
- Unit 4 - Social Inequality and Applied Methods of Sociological Enquiry
- named sociologists and theories
- research design, sampling, ethics and methods
- crime measurement and patterns
- social inequality by class, gender, ethnicity and age
- exam skills, retrieval questions and revision routines

[Download the Year 2 Sociology Study Guide PDF](https://github.com/dlbeadle78/kellynwjec/blob/main/subjects/sociology/study-guide-year-2.pdf)
