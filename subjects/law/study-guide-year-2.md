# WJEC A Level Law - Year 2 Study Guide

Kellyn's confirmed Year 2 route:

- Criminal Law
- Human Rights Law

The guide covers:

- Unit 3 - The Practice of Substantive Law
- Unit 4 - Substantive Law Perspectives
- actus reus, mens rea, causation and strict liability
- murder, manslaughter and non-fatal offences
- theft, robbery and burglary
- insanity, automatism, intoxication, self-defence, duress and attempts
- the Human Rights Act 1998 and ECHR framework
- Articles 8, 10 and 11
- public order, police powers, surveillance, privacy, confidentiality and defamation
- enforcement, judicial review and the European Court of Human Rights
- scenario application for Unit 3 and evaluation skills for Unit 4
- case and authority banks, retrieval questions and revision plans
